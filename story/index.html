The project I'm proudest of

One inbox,
and the email that made something up

A customer service team, a pile of email, and a system that sorts it before anyone opens it. Told in six scenes.

Scene one · The pile
1emails in ten weeks, each opened by a person, each reply written from scratch
Scene two · The question

Could we sort the pile before a person opens it,
and give them a head start on the reply?

Scene three · Four steps, running live on this laptop
new email
1
Sort it
What is it about, and how does she feel?
2
Find the page
Which paragraphs of policy apply?
3
Write a first draft
From those paragraphs only.
4
A person decides
Is the draft safe to hand over?
Some emails skip the draft on purpose. Scan results, health questions, and anyone cancelling because it didn't work go straight to a person for a phone call.
Scene four · The one it got wrong
Muriel Osterbergdraft reply
Switching from basic to plus
Dear Muriel,

You can switch to AlgaeCal Plus and start taking 4 capsules a day, usually 2 with a meal twice a day, as labelled. However, we recommend tapering off your current dose to avoid any potential side effects.

Regarding Strontium Boost, it is not necessary to take it with AlgaeCal Plus to see results…
Nothing in the pages says this.

The model made it up, politely. A check reads every sentence and asks: where did this come from? This one had no answer. The draft was marked failed, and a person wrote the reply.

For the agentsNo more blank screen. Sorted, policy attached, a draft to edit.
For the team leadThe whole week on one screen: what people write about, how they feel, who needs a call. A minute instead of an afternoon.
Scene six · The honest number

Right about three times in four
on emails it has never seen.

Good enough to help. Not good enough to trust blindly. So a person always decides, and the first month of doing this for real would be spent with the people who answer the phone.
