# Shipping and delivery

Source: algaecal.com FAQ and /shipping-returns, read Oct 2026. Demo knowledge base.

## Standard shipping
Free standard shipping on all orders. Contiguous USA and Canada: 3 to 7 business days. International and non contiguous USA (Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico): 7 to 30 business days. PO Box addresses ship by USPS. Express options exist in some countries for a fee.

## Where AlgaeCal ships
USA, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, Ireland, Hong Kong, Switzerland, Lebanon, Luxembourg, Netherlands, New Zealand, Singapore. International orders may be charged local duties or taxes on delivery.

## Currency
Canada is billed in CAD, the UK in GBP, everywhere else in USD. Prices shown on the site are USD.

## Late, lost or damaged
- Late inside the window: ask the customer to allow the full window first, give the tracking link.
- Past the window or tracking stalled: Customer Care can reship or refund. Collect the order number and the shipping address.
- Damaged or wrong item: no need to return it. Customer Care sends a replacement. Ask for a photo if it helps the warehouse.
- Heat: the products are stable at normal shipping temperatures. Omega 3 oil should be refrigerated after opening.
