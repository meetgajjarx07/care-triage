# Products, dosing and how to take them

Source: algaecal.com product pages and FAQ, read Oct 2026. Demo knowledge base.

## Daily doses (as labelled)
- AlgaeCal Plus: 4 capsules a day, usually 2 with a meal twice a day. 30 servings per box.
- AlgaeCal (basic): 3 capsules a day.
- Strontium Boost: 2 capsules a day, taken at a different time from calcium (for example at bedtime), because calcium and strontium compete for absorption. Take on an empty stomach or at least two hours away from the calcium.
- Collagen: 1 scoop (about 12.5 g) in a drink, once a day.
- Magnesium Relax: 2 capsules, usually in the evening.
- D3 Complete: 1 softgel a day.
- Triple Power Omega 3: 1 tablespoon a day. One bottle lasts 29 days at that dose, about 14 days on the double dose protocol. Refrigerate after opening.

## Questions that come up a lot
- Missed a day: just continue the next day, do not double up.
- Splitting the dose: fine, and better for calcium absorption than taking all four at once.
- Thyroid medication: calcium can interfere with levothyroxine absorption. Keep at least four hours between them. Always tell customers to confirm with their doctor or pharmacist.
- With other calcium or a multivitamin: total calcium matters. AlgaeCal Plus has 720 mg of calcium per daily dose. Suggest the customer checks totals with their doctor.
- Capsule size: the capsules are large for some people. They can be opened and the powder mixed into food or a smoothie.
- What is in it: AlgaeCal is a whole food calcium from Lithothamnion algae, with magnesium, vitamin D3, vitamin K2 (MK-7), boron (Plus only) and trace minerals.
- Bone Health Consultants: AlgaeCal has staff who talk customers through their program and DEXA results. They are not medical professionals and say so. Offer a call when a customer has detailed questions about results.
