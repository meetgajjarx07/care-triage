# One year money back guarantee and returns

Source: algaecal.com/guarantee and /shipping-returns, read Oct 2026. Demo knowledge base.

## The policy
Every AlgaeCal product can be returned within one year of purchase for a refund, for any reason. Unopened and even partially used bottles are refunded.

## Steps
1. Customer contacts Customer Care for a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number. The RMA email with instructions goes out within 24 hours.
2. Customer ships the bottles back within one year of the purchase date. Return postage is paid by the customer.
3. Refund is processed within 12 business days of the package arriving. The bank can take up to another 12 business days to show it on the card.

## Rules
- Packages returned without an RMA number are not accepted.
- Refund goes back to the original payment method.
- This policy is separate from the bone density guarantee. A customer does not need DEXA scans to use the one year money back policy.
