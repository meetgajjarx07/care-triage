# Contact channels and when to escalate to a person

Demo knowledge base, Oct 2026.

## Channels
Phone 1-800-820-0184 (toll free USA and Canada), Monday to Friday 5am to 5pm Pacific, weekends 6am to 4pm Pacific. International +1-510-564-7192. Email support@algaecal.com.

## Route to a Bone Health Consultant (human call) instead of an auto reply when
- The email is a guarantee claim with DEXA results attached or described.
- The customer is upset about results after a year or more on the program.
- The customer mentions a medical condition, a new medication or a doctor telling them to stop.
- The customer says they want to cancel because the product did not work. A consultant call recovers some of these; a templated reply recovers almost none.

## Safe to auto draft (still reviewed by a person before sending)
Shipping status, dosing questions covered in the knowledge base, how to pause or change a subscription, rewards points, thank you replies to positive feedback.

## Never
Never promise a refund amount before the account is checked. Never give medical advice. Never tell a customer the scan effect of strontium is zero.
