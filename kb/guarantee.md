# Stronger Bones For Life guarantee

Source: algaecal.com/guarantee, /shipping-returns and the FAQ. Public pages, read Oct 2026. Demo knowledge base, not an official document.

## What it covers
The guarantee covers AlgaeCal Plus taken together with Strontium Boost (sold together as the Bone Builder Pack). AlgaeCal basic, Collagen, D3 Complete, Magnesium Relax and Triple Power are not covered by the bone density guarantee. They fall under the one year money back policy instead.

## The promise
If a customer takes AlgaeCal Plus and Strontium Boost at the full recommended dose for at least six consecutive months and their follow up DEXA scan does not show an increase in bone density in either the hip or the spine, AlgaeCal refunds every penny paid for those two products between the two scans. The promise repeats scan to scan for as long as the customer keeps taking both products as directed.

## The three steps
1. Baseline scan. The customer needs a DEXA scan that is not more than 30 days old when they start. If their last scan is older than 30 days, the guaranteed period starts from their next scan.
2. Full dose for six months or more. Both products, every day, at the dose on the label, for at least six consecutive months and then until the follow up scan. It does not matter if the follow up is at six, twelve or twenty four months as long as the full dose continued between scans.
3. Follow up scan at the same clinic, ideally on the same machine, so the two scans are directly comparable.

## How to claim
Contact Customer Care by phone (1-800-820-0184) or email (support@algaecal.com) with both DEXA reports. The refund is for AlgaeCal Plus and Strontium Boost purchased from AlgaeCal between the two scan dates.

## Common edge cases
- Improvement in one site but not the other: the guarantee is met if hip OR spine increased. A customer whose spine improved and hip stayed flat does not qualify for the refund.
- Different clinic or machine: scans are not directly comparable. Customer Care can still review, but the published terms ask for the same clinic.
- Gaps in dosing or buying only one of the two products: the six consecutive months at full dose on both products is a condition.
- Bought on Amazon or Walmart: the refund is for what was paid to AlgaeCal directly. Marketplace purchases should be raised with Customer Care, who will advise.
- Strontium and DEXA: strontium is heavier than calcium and can make a DEXA reading look a few percent higher than the true change. The company says this on its FAQ. Customers who ask about it deserve a straight answer, not a dodge.
