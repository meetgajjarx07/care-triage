# Subscribe & Save, pausing, cancelling and billing

Source: algaecal.com/sns-terms-conditions and the FAQ, read Oct 2026. Demo knowledge base.

## How it works
Subscribe & Save ships every 30 days and renews automatically until cancelled. The discount varies by product (roughly 8% to 29% off the one time price). A reminder email goes out before each shipment so the customer can skip, change or cancel before it ships.

## Pause, skip, cancel
Customers can pause, skip or cancel at any time. Self service is at checkout.algaecal.com/account/subscriptions (log in, find the subscription, choose pause or change date). Customers can also call 1-800-820-0184 or email support@algaecal.com and Customer Care will do it for them.

## Billing details customers ask about
- Credit cards only. Debit cards are not accepted for subscriptions.
- The price charged follows the current algaecal.com price, so if the site price changes the subscription charge changes too. The reminder email states the new amount.
- Cancelling and later restarting may not restore the same discount.
- If a payment fails the subscription goes on hold and nothing ships until the card is updated.
- Subscribe & Save is only offered to US shipping addresses per the terms page. Canadian customers should be told this plainly and offered the 3 or 6 month one time packs instead.

## Charged twice or unexpected charge
Ask for the order numbers or the last four digits of the card and the dates. Customer Care can refund a duplicate charge. Do not promise a refund amount before the account is checked.

## Rewards and referrals
Customers are enrolled in Rewards automatically. 100 points = $5 off. Points expire if there is no qualifying purchase or point activity for 12 months. Referral: the friend gets a $20 coupon, the referrer gets 400 points once the friend places a first order of $75 or more through the referral link.
